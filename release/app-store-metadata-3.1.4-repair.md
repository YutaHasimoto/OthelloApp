## App Review Notes (English)

Version 3.1.4 is an emergency correction of localized App Store metadata. It replaces identifier-like public app names and restores each locale’s name, subtitle, promotional text, description, keywords, and What’s New text to the intended fields. The app binary and gameplay behavior are unchanged from version 3.1.3.

The app supports Japanese, English, Korean, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, and Simplified Chinese. Spanish (Mexico) temporarily reuses the Spanish (Spain) metadata copy for this emergency repair; the in-app Spanish localization is language-generic.

No account or sign-in is required. The app has no ads and no in-app purchases. Gameplay works offline. StoreKit is used only to request the standard App Store rating prompt after a CPU victory.

CPU difficulty behavior:
- Easy, Normal, and Strong are available from the first launch.
- Oni is visible but locked initially.
- Oni unlocks persistently after the player completes a game on Strong difficulty and defeats the Strong CPU.

No review credentials or special hardware are required.
